# NEXUS — Mission Operations Copilot

> **Evidence-Grounded Intelligence for Space Mission Operations**

NEXUS is a mission operations copilot designed to help operators investigate spacecraft anomalies using **mission telemetry, mission logs, historical incidents, and official operational procedures**.

Instead of simply generating an AI answer, NEXUS builds an **evidence-backed investigation** that clearly separates observed facts from AI recommendations and maintains an auditable timeline of the incident.

---

## 🚀 The Problem

During a spacecraft anomaly, operators need to quickly answer:

- What happened?
- When did it happen?
- What evidence supports the anomaly?
- Which telemetry signals changed?
- Has a similar incident happened before?
- Which official procedure applies?
- What actions should the operator consider?
- Can the entire investigation be audited later?

Traditional monitoring dashboards provide data, but operators still have to manually connect the evidence.

**NEXUS connects the evidence into one operational investigation.**
